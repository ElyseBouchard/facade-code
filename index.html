Unforeseen conditions.

Renovation, cost

On a $1M renovation, surprises add about $35,600 in change orders. The largest share comes from existing conditions nobody could see.

2.1—2.4

Behind the wall.

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing

A finished wall hides studs, ducts, copper, drains and conduit. Facade records what is behind it, bay by bay, so the team can plan before anyone opens it up.

3.1—3.6

The scanner, taken apart.

Hardware, concept

A handheld scanner, rolled along the wall: a thermal camera in the nose, a metal-sensing coil and a bay reserved for radar underneath, and a roller that measures distance as it goes.

4.1—4.4

Field evidence.

Software, review

Every reading lands on the wall where it was taken. A detection is a lead, not a verdict: a person reviews it before it changes the plan.
